Artificial intelligence is transforming healthcare by improving diagnostics,
speeding up medical imaging analysis, and helping doctors identify patterns in
large patient datasets. In finance, AI is used for fraud detection,
algorithmic trading, customer support, and risk assessment. In robotics, AI
enables machines to perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and adapt to
dynamic environments.

At the same time, AI systems raise concerns about privacy, fairness,
accountability, and the need for human oversight. In education, AI can
personalize learning experiences and provide intelligent tutoring systems. In
manufacturing, it supports predictive maintenance, quality control, and supply
chain optimization.
